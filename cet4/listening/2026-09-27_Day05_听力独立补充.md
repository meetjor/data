## 三、只对照两个定位句

### 定位句1

> Kirsteen Marshall ... posted a tearful video on social media, begging for the safe return of her beloved pet dog.

中文：Kirsteen 在社交媒体上发布了一段含泪视频，请求她心爱的宠物狗平安回来。

分块：
- posted a tearful video
- on social media
- begging for...
- the safe return of...
- her beloved pet dog

重点不是 social media，而是后面的 safe return of her pet dog。

### 定位句2

> The family is now offering a £5,000 reward for the safe return of the dog.

中文：这家人现在悬赏5000英镑，希望狗能安全回来。

分块：
- The family is now
- offering
- a £5,000 reward
- for the safe return
- of the dog

重点听 offering + reward。
