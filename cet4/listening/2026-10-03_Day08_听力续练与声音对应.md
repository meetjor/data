### News Report 2 只处理以下两处

**Kirsteen Marshall ... posted a tearful video on social media, begging for the safe return of her beloved pet dog.**

省略号表示节选，不是完整新闻原文。

- posted a tearful video / on social media：在社交媒体发布一段含泪视频。
- begging for / the safe return / of her beloved pet dog：请求心爱的宠物狗平安回来。
- 动作与目的分开：发视频是行动，希望狗回来才是目的。

**The family is now offering a £5,000 reward for the safe return of the dog.**

- The family is now offering / a £5,000 reward / for the safe return of the dog.
- 中文：这家人悬赏5000英镑，希望狗平安回来。
- 抓offer a reward，不只记住数字。

### News Report 3 仅在选择这篇时使用

**Owner Guy Wilson says his café aims to build community, rather than profits.**

- aims to / build community / rather than profits。
- 目的在于建立社区联系，而不是追求利润。rather than不能漏听。

**The café doesn't use volunteers, but pays its staff to ensure consistency in its service.**

- doesn't use volunteers / but pays its staff / to ensure consistency in its service。
- 不用志愿者，而给员工付薪，以保持服务一致；注意doesn't与but。

**Most people will have something in common with each other.**

- have something in common / with each other。
- 人们彼此有共同点，整块识别have something in common。

</details>

## 关原文复听 3分钟

关闭文字，把两个或三个定位片段各听两次。先听完整块，再写中文意思，不要求写完整英文。

1. 我听出的动作：______
2. 我听出的目的或结果：______
3. 还需要看文字的片段：______

## 跟读 2分钟

只跟所选新闻的关键块，每块两次。先跟意思和停顿，再练速度；不用模仿每个口音细节。

- News 2：begging for / the safe return；offering / a £5,000 reward。
- News 3：aims to build community；doesn't use volunteers；have something in common。
