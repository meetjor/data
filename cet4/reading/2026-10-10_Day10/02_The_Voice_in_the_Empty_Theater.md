# The Voice in the Empty Theater

原创可选短文，不是四级真题。正文179词。第一遍先抓声音从哪里来，不逐句翻译。

A theater worker named Bo heard applause from an empty room. The evening show had ended an hour earlier. He checked every row, but found nobody.

The sound stopped. Then it began again, louder this time.

Bo called his friend Mei, who managed the lights. "Someone is still here," he said. "They are enjoying a show I cannot see." Mei followed him into the room. Together, they checked the stage and the space behind the curtains.

Finally, Mei noticed a small bag under a seat. Inside was a phone. Every five minutes, it played the sound of a hundred people clapping.

The next morning, its owner returned. He was an actor practicing for his first public performance. He had set the applause as a reminder to finish each part of his speech.

"Did it work?" Bo asked.

"Very well," the actor said. "I finished early. But I forgot my bag." Bo returned the phone and asked him to choose a quieter reminder next time. The actor smiled. Then, for the first time that night, the theater was truly silent.

## 上下文猜词

applause附近有a hundred people clapping；curtains与舞台相连；reminder在每五分钟响一次、提示结束演讲部分的语境中。先猜再核对。
