# The Piano That Ordered Soup

原创兴趣故事，不是四级真题。正文318词；建议第一遍不查词，先读懂“琴键为什么能点餐”。可选输入，不计入正式PDF必做题量。

Jun found a small cafe beside the sea. Near the window stood an old piano with a sign: "Please play." He ordered tea, sat down, and pressed one key to check the sound.

A bell rang in the kitchen. A minute later, the cook appeared with a bowl of tomato soup.

"I did not order this," Jun said. "I only touched the piano." The cook looked at the bowl, then at the piano. "That is strange," she replied.

Jun tried another key. This time, a green light flashed above the kitchen door. The cook returned with bread. When he pressed the pedal under the piano, she brought a spoon.

The owner, Ms Wu, came downstairs and started laughing. Years ago, the cafe had been a restaurant with very loud music. Staff needed a quiet way to share orders with the kitchen. They had connected two piano keys and a pedal to colored lights. One key meant soup, another meant bread, and the pedal meant a spoon.

The system had saved time, but the restaurant later became a cafe. Most of the wires were removed. Nobody remembered the last three.

Jun was puzzled. He had played this kind of piano before, but it had never provided him with lunch.

Ms Wu put paper over the two special keys. "Now you can play safely," she said. Jun chose a quiet tune. For several minutes, everything went well.

Then a little boy joined him. He pointed at the paper. "Why are those keys sleeping?" Before anyone could explain the rule, he lifted the paper and pressed both.

In the kitchen, the cook looked up from a saucepan. The red and green lights were flashing together.

Ms Wu planned to repair the system the next morning. For now, she changed the sign: "Please play. Soup may follow." Jun finished his tea, shared the bread with the boy, and asked for another spoon.

## 读完再回看

pedal的位置在钢琴下方；puzzled附近说明Jun以前从未用钢琴得到午餐；tune与play相连；saucepan在厨房语境中。先猜意思，再展开核对。

## 旧词与新结构自然复现

share orders、save time、provide him with lunch、join、explain the rule、plan to repair；Most of the wires were removed只需要理解“多数线已经被拆掉”，不要求另加被动专项。

**Soup may follow.** 汤可能会随后送来。may表示可能，不能读成每次都一定发生。故事最后的告示保留了这个小玩笑。
