# The Umbrella with a Job

原创兴趣阅读｜190词｜不是历年真题。先读故事，再看辅助词。

At a small hotel, a guest borrowed an umbrella from the front desk. A label on it said, “Please return me. I know where you are staying.”

He laughed and took a photograph.

The next morning, he noticed the same message on a phone charger. A library book in the lounge carried another label: “You may finish the story here. You may not take me home.”

He asked the receptionist who wrote them.

“My father,” she said. “He used to put serious notices everywhere, but nobody read them.”

“Does this method work?”

She opened a drawer containing twelve umbrellas.

“People return ours now. Sometimes they return other hotels' umbrellas too.”

Before leaving, the guest returned his umbrella and borrowed a pen to sign a form. He put the pen in his pocket without thinking.

At the station, he discovered a tiny label wrapped around it.

“Nice pocket,” it said. “But I work at the hotel.”

He went back. The receptionist thanked him and offered him the pen as a gift.

He refused politely.

“I would feel,” he explained, “as if I were taking someone away from a job they enjoy.”
