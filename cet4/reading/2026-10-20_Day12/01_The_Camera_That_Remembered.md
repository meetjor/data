# The Camera That Remembered

原创兴趣阅读｜307词｜不是历年真题。先读故事，再看辅助词。

When a second-hand shop received an old camera, the owner found a roll of film inside. A customer offered to buy the camera, but wanted the film removed first.

“There may be somebody's holiday on it,” said the owner.

She asked a local photographer to develop the pictures. Most were ordinary: a street, a kitchen, a dog asleep beside a broken chair. The last photograph showed a man holding a handwritten sign. It said, “If you find this camera, please repair the clock.”

Behind him stood a tall clock with no hands.

The owner posted the picture online, hoping someone could explain it. People suggested a secret message, a hidden fortune or a joke. One person carefully compared the wallpaper with pictures of local houses.

Then an elderly woman called.

“That's my brother,” she said. “And the clock belongs to me.”

She came to the shop carrying a small envelope. Inside were two clock hands.

Years earlier, her brother had borrowed her camera to photograph things he wanted to repair. He had removed the clock hands, put them somewhere safe and forgotten where. The woman had found them in his coat after he moved away.

“I kept them because he always promised to return,” she explained.

The shop owner asked whether her brother had finally repaired the clock.

“Oh, he visits every Sunday,” the woman said. “He just brings cake instead.”

They decided to send him the last photograph. On the back, the owner wrote, “The camera works. Your sister is still waiting for the clock.”

The following Sunday, the brother arrived with a box of tools and a cake. He repaired the clock in twenty minutes.

“Why did it take you twenty years?” his sister asked.

“I couldn't confirm where the hands were,” he said.

She placed the empty envelope beside his plate. “Next time, ask.”
