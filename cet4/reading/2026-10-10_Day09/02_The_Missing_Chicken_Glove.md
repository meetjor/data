# The Missing Chicken Glove

原创可选短文，不是四级真题。正文189词，建议6–8分钟；先理解故事转折。

The train station had a box for lost gloves. Most were black, gray, or brown. Then someone left a bright yellow glove shaped like a chicken.

Rui, who worked at the station, wanted to find its owner. She put a photograph on the station's public page. "One yellow glove is waiting for its other half," she wrote.

The next morning, a woman arrived with a matching glove. It was yellow too, but it looked like a duck.

"These do not match," Rui said.

"They do," the woman replied. "My son chose them. One hand is a chicken, and the other is a duck."

She showed Rui a photograph of the boy wearing both. That was enough evidence.

Before they left, the boy waved both hands. The animals appeared to greet each other. Rui laughed and returned the missing glove.

Later, she changed her online message: "The chicken has found the duck. Both are going home."

People had offered many guesses about the glove. Nobody had guessed the right answer. Rui was glad she had asked for a photograph rather than deciding that every pair must look exactly the same.

## 猜词

1. matching：另一只手套怎样与遗失的手套联系起来？
2. pair：结合both hands猜词义。

## 理解题

1. Why did Rui return the glove to the woman?
- [ ] A. The woman showed a photograph of the boy wearing both gloves.
- [ ] B. The two gloves looked exactly the same.
- [ ] C. The woman worked at the station.
- [ ] D. The boy promised to buy another pair.

2. Which idea did the story challenge?
- [ ] A. Photographs can provide useful evidence.
- [ ] B. Lost things should be returned.
- [ ] C. A pair of gloves must always look identical.
- [ ] D. Public notices can help people.

## 旧词复现

find、public、evidence、offer、rather than。

<details><summary>答案 词义与中文概要</summary>

1 A；2 C。

matching（adj.）：相配的；pair（n.可数）：一双、一对。文中并非说外观相同，而是男孩选的一双搭配。

概要：遗失的鸡形手套与鸭形手套原本就是男孩的一双。Rui看过照片才确认归属，没有只靠“同一双应该长得一样”的猜测。
