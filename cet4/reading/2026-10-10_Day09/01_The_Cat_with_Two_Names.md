# The Cat with Two Names

原创学习故事，不是四级真题。正文309词，建议12–15分钟；第一遍不查词、不逐句翻译。

When a white cat appeared outside his window, Chen offered it a little water. The cat drank, looked at him, and walked away. The next evening, it returned at exactly six. Chen called it Snow.

A week later, he saw a public notice near the village shop. It showed a white cat called Cloud. "Lost for three days," the notice said. Chen was almost sure that Cloud was Snow.

He phoned the owner, Mrs Lin, and gave her two suggestions: come at six, and bring a photograph. Comparing the photograph with the cat seemed better than trusting his memory.

Mrs Lin arrived carrying a small collar, a band that a cat wears around its neck. Snow was already on the doorstep, just outside Chen's front door. However, it did not come when she called "Cloud."

"Perhaps it likes its new name," Chen said.

Mrs Lin studied the photograph. "Cloud has two white front feet. This cat has only one."

Chen looked more carefully. The other front foot was gray. He had missed that detail every evening.

Then they heard a soft scratch behind the garden gate. Something was rubbing a sharp foot against the wood. Mrs Lin opened the gate, and another white cat walked in. This one had two white front feet.

For a moment, neither person spoke. Then Chen laughed. He had been leaving the gate open because he thought one cat was coming and going. In fact, two cats had been visiting his garden at different times.

Cloud went straight to Mrs Lin. Snow stayed beside the water bowl and looked perfectly pleased.

Before leaving, Mrs Lin removed the lost-cat notice. Chen made a new one: "Two cats, two names. Please pay attention to their feet."

He still offered Snow water. But now, before calling a name, he checked the feet. It saved everyone some confusion.

## 猜词

1. collar：后面的a band…解释了什么？
2. doorstep：just outside Chen’s front door说明在哪里？
3. scratch：结合爪子蹭木头，猜声音或动作。
4. confusion：两只猫被当成一只，造成什么？

## 理解题

1. Why did Chen ask Mrs Lin to bring a photograph?
- [ ] A. To make a new advertisement for the shop.
- [ ] B. To compare details instead of relying on memory.
- [ ] C. To teach Snow to answer to a new name.
- [ ] D. To find out what food the cat liked.

2. What detail helped distinguish the two cats?
- [ ] A. The color of their front feet.
- [ ] B. The size of their water bowls.
- [ ] C. The time the shop opened.
- [ ] D. The sound of their names.

3. What had Chen misunderstood?
- [ ] A. Mrs Lin had no cat.
- [ ] B. Snow was afraid of photographs.
- [ ] C. Two cats were actually one cat.
- [ ] D. Two cats had been visiting at different times.

## 旧词与结构复现

offer、public、suggestion、compare、trust、memory、detail、pay attention to、save；Comparing the photograph…作主语，不能读成“照片在比较”。

1 B；2 A；3 D。

collar（n.可数）：项圈；doorstep（n.可数）：门阶、门口；scratch（n.可数/v.）：抓挠声、抓挠；confusion（n.不可数）：混淆。

概要：Chen以为常来花园的是一只猫。失主拿照片核对前脚颜色后，两人才发现有两只相似的白猫。另一只才是失主寻找的Cloud。
