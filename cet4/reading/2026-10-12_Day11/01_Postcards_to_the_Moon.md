# Postcards to the Moon

原创兴趣阅读｜317词｜2026-10-12 Day11。可选输入，不是四级真题。先不查词，读情节；生词先从上下文猜，不加整篇默写。

When a small town replaced its old bus shelter, the workers found a narrow wooden box behind the notice board. Inside were twenty-seven postcards. Each card had the same address: The Moon.

The post office manager, Nora, thought it was a joke. Still, she checked the dates carefully. The cards had been written over thirty years, usually on rainy Mondays. None had a name.

Some messages were simple. "The bakery has changed its window again." Others sounded like warnings. "Do not land near the football field. The ground is too soft."

One card contained a small map of the town. A library, a bridge and a bus stop were marked with stars. Nora decided to follow the route after work.

The walk ended at a tiny house beside the river. An elderly woman opened the door. When Nora showed her the cards, the woman smiled and brought out a photograph of two children wearing cardboard helmets.

"My brother and I planned to visit the moon," she explained. "Then he became a bus driver. I worked at the library. We never left this town."

"Were these cards for him?"

"Yes. He called his bus the Moon because it was round, white and always late. I hid a card at the shelter whenever he had a difficult week. After reading it, he put it back."

The old bus had gone years ago. Her brother now lived in another city, and the cards had remained behind the board.

Nora asked whether she should post them. The woman shook her head. "He has read every one. Keep them somewhere people can see them."

At the new shelter, Nora placed the cards under glass, beside the current bus information. A child studied the address and asked when the bus to the moon would arrive.

"Not today," Nora said. "But if you wait five minutes, this one will take you to the library."
