# Probably Art

原创兴趣阅读｜184词｜2026-10-12 Day11。可选输入，不是四级真题。先不查词，读情节；生词先从上下文猜，不加整篇默写。

A visitor at a small art museum stopped in front of a painting of an empty chair. A label beside it said, "Please sit down."

There was no chair in the room.

He asked the manager whether something was missing. She looked at the painting, checked her notes and called the artist. The artist laughed for so long that the manager put the phone on speaker.

"The label belongs to the bench near the entrance," he said. "Someone moved it. My painting is called Waiting for the Bus."

The visitor felt foolish until he noticed another label: "Do not touch."

It stood beside an ordinary umbrella.

The manager frowned. She had left the umbrella there that morning. It was not part of the exhibition.

They walked around the museum together. Five labels were in the wrong places. On a low table, a new label read, "My First Exhibition."

Behind the table sat the manager's six-year-old son, cutting paper with great care.

"I improved the information," he explained. "Now everything has a name."

The visitor bought one of his paper labels. It said, "Probably Art."
