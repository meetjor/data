## 今日两个结构

原因句用于巩固that后的完整句；By doing so是本次新增的行动结果结构。

**One reason is that + 完整句**：一个原因是……。that后必须有自己的主语和谓语。

- One reason is that **technology can save time**.
- One reason is that **checking information can help us avoid mistakes**.

第二句中checking information整体是主语；不能写students check information can…。

**By doing so, + 主语 + can + 动词原形**：通过这样做，……可以……。doing so指回前句的具体行动。

- Students check their work carefully. **By doing so, they can find mistakes.**

前句必须说清行动；不要没有上文就用By doing so。can后用原形find，不写finds。

## 简化范文

### Is technology making people lazy?

Technology can help people, but its effects depend on how we use it. One reason is that it can save time and offer useful information.

However, using technology should not replace thinking. Students should compare information from different sources and check their work carefully. By doing so, they can find mistakes and make better decisions.

Checking their work can also help them pay attention to details. Technology is a useful tool. We should make good use of it rather than depend too much on it.

## 中文译文

科技能够帮助人们，但它的影响取决于我们如何使用它。一个原因是，它可以节省时间并提供有用的信息。
然而，使用科技不应取代思考。学生应该比较不同来源的信息，并认真检查自己的作业。通过这样做，他们可以发现错误并作出更好的决定。
检查作业也能帮助他们注意细节。科技是有用的工具。我们应该充分利用它，而不是过度依赖它。

## 值得学的表达

- its effects depend on how we use it — 它的影响取决于我们如何使用它。
- One reason is that… — 引出支持观点的一个理由。
- By doing so, they can… — 说明前面行动带来的结果。
- Checking their work can help them… — 动名词短语作主语，help后用宾格them。
- make good use of it rather than depend too much on it — 充分利用它，而不是过度依赖它。

## 少量补充表达

- replace /rɪˈpleɪs/ **v.** 取代：replace thinking — 取代思考。先会认，不新增默写要求。
- think for themselves — 独立思考。以词组理解；themselves为反身代词。
- offer useful information — 提供有用的信息。information不可数。

今天至少可迁移一句：**One reason is that checking information can help us avoid mistakes.** 换成checking their work等自己的内容，不能只抄整篇。
