# 17 快递服务的影响

任务类型：发展与影响｜正文158词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

介绍快递服务的发展如何影响生活，同时说明收益、问题和改进方向。

真题任务参照：2024年12月快递行业发展影响任务类型；原创类题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## The Effects of Delivery Services

**Delivery** services have changed the way many **consumers** **purchase** everyday goods. A useful product can reach a **household** without a long shopping trip, and electronic **commerce** has created new forms of **employment**. These benefits are especially clear when people need items that local shops do not provide.

However, convenience also brings **expense** and **pressure**. Each **package** needs materials, transportation and someone to deliver it. More **vehicles** may add to traffic, while unnecessary packaging can have a **negative** effect on the environment. Workers may also face difficult schedules when customers expect every order to arrive immediately. A low price does not show every cost involved in the service.

Despite these problems, delivery can remain a valuable part of modern life. Consumers can combine orders and avoid unnecessary purchases. Companies should improve worker protection and use materials more carefully. Public authorities can **regulate** unsafe practices without preventing useful development. The aim should be a service that is convenient and responsible.


## 中文理解


快递服务改变了许多消费者购买日用品的方式。有用商品不需要长途购物便能送到家庭，电子商务也创造新就业形式。人们需要本地商店不提供的物品时，这些收益尤其明显。


不过，便利也带来费用和压力。每个包裹需要材料、运输和配送人员。更多车辆可能增加交通负担，不必要包装会对环境产生负面影响。消费者期待每笔订单立即送达时，工作人员也可能面临困难的安排。低价并未显示服务涉及的每项成本。


尽管存在问题，快递仍能是现代生活中有价值的一部分。消费者可以合并订单、避免不必要购买；公司应加强劳动者保护、谨慎使用材料；公共部门可以规范不安全做法，同时不阻碍有益发展。目标应是便利而负责任的服务。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 交代变化影响

**骨架：** ...has/have changed the way + 人 + 动词

> Delivery services have changed the way many consumers purchase everyday goods.

services复数配have；the way后说购买方式，不只说生活变了。

### 2. 让步后给判断

**骨架：** Despite + 名词短语, 完整句

> Despite these problems, delivery can remain a valuable part of modern life.

despite后不用直接接these problems exist完整句。
