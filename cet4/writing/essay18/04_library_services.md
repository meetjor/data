# 04 改善图书馆服务

任务类型：提案与措施｜正文149词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

以学生身份提出改善图书馆服务的具体措施，写清问题、行动和效果。

真题任务参照：2022年6月改善校园服务任务；原创方案。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Improving Library Services

Our library has useful books, but its services could become more convenient. A common **complaint** is that students cannot easily find a book or learn whether a seat is available. I suggest that the university improve its **digital** **catalogue** and publish a clear opening **schedule**.

An **updated** catalogue would help students **locate** materials before visiting the building. A simple system could also allow them to **reserve** a seat for a limited period. **Staff** should explain the rules clearly so that unused reservations do not keep other students waiting. **Adequate** signs inside the library would guide visitors to the right floor without repeated questions.

The university should collect **feedback** and **respond** to problems regularly. It could begin with a short student survey rather than an expensive redesign. These measures would improve **convenience** while protecting equal access. Better service means helping students use existing resources effectively, not simply buying more books.


## 中文理解


图书馆有有用的书，但服务还能更方便。常见抱怨是学生不易找到书，也不易知道是否有座位。我建议学校完善数字目录并公布清楚的开放时间表。


更新后的目录能帮助学生到馆前找到资料。简单的系统也能让他们在限定时间内预约座位。工作人员应解释规则，避免空预约让其他学生等待。馆内足够的标识能引导访客到正确楼层，减少反复询问。


学校应定期收集反馈并回应问题。可以先做一次简短学生调查，而不是昂贵地重新设计。措施能提升便利性并保障公平使用。更好的服务意味着帮助学生有效使用现有资源，而不只是购买更多书。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 提出明确建议

**骨架：** I suggest that + 主语 + 原形/should原形

> I suggest that the university improve its digital catalogue and publish a clear opening schedule.

建议从句中improve、publish用原形；基础练习也可保留should。

### 2. 解释措施效果

**骨架：** ...would help + 人 + 原形

> An updated catalogue would help students locate materials before visiting the building.

句首措施是帮助的来源，locate是学生执行的动作。
