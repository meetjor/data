# 01 开放校园体育设施

任务类型：观点与立场｜正文145词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

以大学生身份说明是否支持校园体育设施向公众开放，并给出理由和条件。

真题任务参照：2024年6月三套校园设施开放立场任务；本题为原创类题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Opening Campus Sports Facilities

I support opening some campus sports **facilities** to local **residents** if student needs come first. Public **access** can bring a clear **benefit** to the community. People can exercise near home, and the university can build a better relationship with its neighbors. However, a campus is also a place for academic work.

The main **concern** is **capacity**. When a gym is full, students may lose their usual training time. The university should therefore **restrict** public visits to hours when enough space is **available**. Visitors should ask for **permission** before entering **private** areas and follow the same safety rules as students. Clear signs and a simple booking system would help everyone understand the limits.

Opening every building without conditions would be inappropriate. An **appropriate** policy should share useful resources while protecting normal campus life. Both the university and visitors must accept **responsibility** for making the arrangement work.


## 中文理解


如果学生需求得到优先保障，我支持向当地居民开放部分校园体育设施。公众使用这些设施能给社区带来明确的好处。人们可以就近运动，大学也能与邻里建立更好的关系。不过，校园也是开展学业活动的地方。


主要顾虑是容纳能力。体育馆满员时，学生可能失去平时的训练时间。因此，学校应把公众到访限制在空间充足的时段。访客进入非开放区域前应获得许可，并遵守与学生相同的安全规定。清楚的标识和简单的预约系统能帮助大家理解限制。


无条件开放所有建筑是不合适的。恰当的政策应在共享有用资源的同时保护正常校园生活。学校和访客都必须承担责任，让安排真正可行。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 有条件地支持

**骨架：** I support + doing/noun + if + 完整句

> I support opening some campus sports facilities to local residents if student needs come first.

先写支持的做法，再用完整if句给条件，条件必须能解决顾虑。

### 2. 把措施限定在合理范围

**骨架：** restrict + 对象 + to + 范围

> The university should therefore restrict public visits to hours when enough space is available.

restrict A to B：把A限制在B；不只空泛地说加强管理。

# 02 合理使用AI辅助学习

任务类型：观点与立场｜正文154词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

评论大学生如何恰当使用AI辅助学习，说明收益、风险和使用原则。

真题任务参照：2025年6月AI辅助学习任务类型；采用原创情境，不揭露保留卷原题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Using AI as a Study Assistant

**Artificial** intelligence can be an **efficient** assistant, but it should not replace **independent** thinking. Students may use it to understand an unfamiliar idea or organize a study plan. This can leave more time for difficult **academic** tasks. The value of the tool depends on how we use it.

An answer that sounds confident is not always **accurate**. AI may **mislead** students by presenting incorrect information as a fact. It is important to check the **source** and look for **reliable** **evidence** before accepting an answer. Comparing different explanations can also help students notice mistakes. When writing an assignment, they should produce an **original** response rather than copy a complete answer.

**Responsible** use requires clear limits. AI can **assist** with preparation, while students remain responsible for the final work. They should be able to explain their own ideas without the tool. Used in this way, AI can support learning instead of becoming a shortcut around it.


## 中文理解


人工智能可以成为高效的助手，但不应代替独立思考。学生可以用它理解陌生概念或安排学习计划。这能给困难的学业任务留出更多时间。工具的价值取决于我们如何使用它。


听起来很自信的答案不一定准确。AI可能把错误信息当作事实呈现，从而误导学生。接受答案前，核查来源并寻找可靠证据很重要。比较不同解释也能帮助学生发现错误。写作业时，学生应作出原创回应，而不是复制完整答案。


负责任地使用需要明确限制。AI可以协助准备，但学生仍须对最终作业负责。他们应能在不用工具的情况下解释自己的想法。这样使用，AI就能支持学习，而不是成为绕过学习的捷径。

### 1. 承认收益后给限制

**骨架：** ...can..., but it should not...

> Artificial intelligence can be an efficient assistant, but it should not replace independent thinking.

but两边都完整；限制对应学习目的，不用一刀切。

> It is important to check the source and look for reliable evidence before accepting an answer.

两个动作check、look并列；before后accepting是动名词。

# 03 必修课程与学生选择

任务类型：观点与立场｜正文148词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

讨论大学应如何在基础必修课程与学生选择之间取得平衡，明确立场和理由。

真题任务参照：2025年6月必修课必要性及2025年12月学术写作要求等立场任务；原创类题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Core Courses and Student Choice

A university **curriculum** should **combine** **essential** courses with **flexible** choices. Some common subjects give students the knowledge they need to become **competent** in their field. Others allow them to explore an interest and make a better **career** decision. These two purposes are both valuable.

**Professional** **qualifications** and **employment** requirements deserve attention. A student preparing for a particular occupation may need a clear foundation before taking advanced courses. At the same time, different students have different **ambitions**. Requiring everyone to follow exactly the same path may discourage useful exploration. The university could offer a small core and several **options**, with advice on how each option supports later study or work.

Choice does not mean avoiding every difficult subject. Students should explain their decisions and develop the **discipline** to finish what they start. With clear guidance, they can choose responsibly while building the abilities that their future work will require.


## 中文理解


大学课程体系应结合必要课程与灵活选择。一些共同课程提供学生胜任专业所需的知识，另一些让学生探索兴趣并作出更好的职业决定。这两个目的都有价值。


专业资格和就业要求值得重视。准备从事某种职业的学生，学习高阶课程前可能需要明确的基础。同时，不同学生有不同的志向。要求所有人遵循完全相同的道路可能妨碍有益的探索。学校可以提供一小组核心课程及若干选项，并说明每个选项如何支持后续学习或工作。


选择不等于避开所有困难科目。学生应该解释自己的决定，并培养完成所开始任务的自律。在明确指导下，他们能负责任地选择，同时建立未来工作所需的能力。

**骨架：** ...does not mean + doing

> Choice does not mean avoiding every difficult subject.

mean后接avoiding这一行动；不是mean avoid。

## Learning to Communicate

Good **communication** does not mean speaking more than everyone else. It means listening carefully, expressing an idea clearly and checking whether others understand it. These abilities support **collaboration** in class and help students handle **conflict** outside the classroom.

**Mutual** respect and **patience** are especially important when people disagree. Instead of quickly blaming someone, we can ask a question to **clarify** the problem. We should also **acknowledge** a useful point in another person's opinion. An **attitude** that is **sensitive** to other people's feelings can prevent a small misunderstanding from becoming a larger argument. When students **interact** with people from different backgrounds, **tolerance** helps them learn without expecting everyone to behave in the same way.

Practice can begin with an ordinary group task. Each member explains an idea, listens to comments and helps make a clear decision. Such experience can build **confidence** over time. Communication improves when students focus on solving a shared problem rather than winning every conversation.

# 17 快递服务的影响

任务类型：发展与影响｜正文158词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

介绍快递服务的发展如何影响生活，同时说明收益、问题和改进方向。

真题任务参照：2024年12月快递行业发展影响任务类型；原创类题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## The Effects of Delivery Services

**Delivery** services have changed the way many **consumers** **purchase** everyday goods. A useful product can reach a **household** without a long shopping trip, and electronic **commerce** has created new forms of **employment**. These benefits are especially clear when people need items that local shops do not provide.

However, convenience also brings **expense** and **pressure**. Each **package** needs materials, transportation and someone to deliver it. More **vehicles** may add to traffic, while unnecessary packaging can have a **negative** effect on the environment. Workers may also face difficult schedules when customers expect every order to arrive immediately. A low price does not show every cost involved in the service.

Despite these problems, delivery can remain a valuable part of modern life. Consumers can combine orders and avoid unnecessary purchases. Companies should improve worker protection and use materials more carefully. Public authorities can **regulate** unsafe practices without preventing useful development. The aim should be a service that is convenient and responsible.


## 中文理解


快递服务改变了许多消费者购买日用品的方式。有用商品不需要长途购物便能送到家庭，电子商务也创造新就业形式。人们需要本地商店不提供的物品时，这些收益尤其明显。


不过，便利也带来费用和压力。每个包裹需要材料、运输和配送人员。更多车辆可能增加交通负担，不必要包装会对环境产生负面影响。消费者期待每笔订单立即送达时，工作人员也可能面临困难的安排。低价并未显示服务涉及的每项成本。


尽管存在问题，快递仍能是现代生活中有价值的一部分。消费者可以合并订单、避免不必要购买；公司应加强劳动者保护、谨慎使用材料；公共部门可以规范不安全做法，同时不阻碍有益发展。目标应是便利而负责任的服务。

### 1. 交代变化影响

**骨架：** Despite + 名词短语, 完整句

> Despite these problems, delivery can remain a valuable part of modern life.

despite后不用直接接these problems exist完整句。

# 18 机器人未来的校园应用

任务类型：未来应用｜正文161词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

描述机器人未来可能在校园承担的工作、潜在收益及使用边界。

真题任务参照：2026年6月机器人校园应用任务类型；只设计原创场景，不展示保留卷原题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Robots on Campus

In the future, **robots** may be used to **assist** with simple campus tasks. An **automatic** **device** could carry books between buildings or guide visitors to a classroom. Its main **function** would be to handle repeated work, leaving staff more time for tasks that require personal attention.

The **potential** benefits are useful, but they do not remove the need for human control. Robots require **maintenance**, and a broken device may cause delays instead of saving time. **Engineering** students could help test simple systems, while trained staff **supervise** daily use. **Security** rules should also limit what information a robot collects and explain who can access it.

Robots should not **replace** every service provided by people. Human **judgment** remains important when a student is upset or a problem has no clear answer. A careful trial would help the university find suitable uses before introducing more devices. Technology should support campus life by solving a real problem, not simply make the campus appear more advanced.


## 中文理解


未来，机器人可能协助简单校园任务。自动设备可以在建筑间运书，或引导访客到教室。主要功能是处理重复工作，让工作人员有更多时间处理需要个人关注的任务。


潜在收益有用，但并未消除人工控制的需要。机器人需要维护，损坏设备可能造成延误而不是省时。工程专业学生可以帮助测试简单系统，受训工作人员监督日常使用。安全规则也应限制机器人收集的信息，并说明谁可访问。


机器人不应替代人提供的所有服务。学生情绪低落或问题没有明确答案时，人的判断仍很重要。谨慎试用能帮助学校找到合适用途，再引进更多设备。科技应通过解决真实问题支持校园生活，而不只是让校园显得更先进。

### 1. 描述可能用途

> In the future, robots may be used to assist with simple campus tasks.

### 2. 限制适用范围

**骨架：** ...should not replace...

> Robots should not replace every service provided by people.

以具体不适用场景支撑边界，不只空喊保留人工。
