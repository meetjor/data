# 10 家乡的变化

任务类型：描述与经历｜正文156词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

向校报描述家乡近年的变化，给具体前后对比并作评价。

真题任务参照：2023年12月家乡新近变化描述任务；原创示例。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Changes in My Hometown

There used to be a narrow road beside the river in my hometown. Today, it has become part of a wider **transportation** network, with safer paths for walking and cycling. The change is one example of the **gradual** improvement in our **local** environment.

New **housing** has appeared near the town center, and workers have **constructed** a small park on an unused site. More **residents** now have a place to relax after work. These changes reflect **economic** development, but they have also raised questions about what should be **preserved**. Some **historical** buildings and familiar shops help people remember the town's past. Replacing them all would make the **landscape** less distinctive.

**Urban** development should improve life without removing every sign of an older **settlement**. In my view, the best changes make daily activities easier while keeping important local features. My hometown is becoming more convenient, and I hope its history will remain visible as it continues to grow.


## 中文理解


家乡河边过去有一条窄路，如今它成为更广交通网络的一部分，并有更安全的步行和骑行道路。这是当地环境逐步改善的一个例子。


镇中心附近出现新住房，工人在闲置场地建了一座小公园。更多居民下班后有了放松场所。变化反映经济发展，但也带来应保留什么的问题。一些历史建筑和熟悉商店帮助人们记住小镇过去。全部替换会让景观失去特色。


城市发展应改善生活，而不抹去旧聚居地的所有痕迹。我认为，最好的变化让日常活动更容易，同时保留当地重要特色。家乡正变得更方便，我希望它继续成长时，历史仍清楚可见。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 交代过去状况

**骨架：** There used to be + 名词

> There used to be a narrow road beside the river in my hometown.

used to be说过去曾有，不等于be used to doing习惯于。

### 2. 评价变化的原则

**骨架：** ...should...without + doing

> Urban development should improve life without removing every sign of an older settlement.

without后用动名词；评价来自前面新建与保留的具体对比。
