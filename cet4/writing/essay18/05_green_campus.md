# 05 共建绿色校园

任务类型：提案与措施｜正文155词｜原创学习范文｜2026-10-14。

## 审题

向学校提出绿色校园措施，覆盖具体行动、实施者及预期帮助。

真题任务参照：2024年12月绿色校园做法任务类型；原创方案。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Building a Greener Campus

A greener campus needs practical changes in everyday habits. The university can start by reducing unnecessary **consumption** and making **recycling** easier. These actions may lower **carbon** emissions and help students understand **environmental** protection through their own experience.

First, **electrical** equipment should be turned off when rooms are empty. Clear reminders near the doors would help students and staff **maintain** this habit. Second, the university could place recycling boxes beside ordinary bins and explain what belongs in each box. Students would then be less likely to **discard** useful materials with other waste. A small **campaign** could show how similar rules work for **household** rubbish.

**Conservation** should continue after the posters disappear. Student groups can check whether the facilities are being used correctly and report problems. Sharing **resources** is another useful step, especially for items used only occasionally. Small actions will not solve every **pollution** problem, but regular participation can make the campus cleaner and less wasteful.


## 中文理解


更绿色的校园需要改变日常习惯。学校可以从减少不必要的消耗、让回收更容易入手。这些行动可能降低碳排放，并让学生通过自身经历理解环境保护。


首先，教室无人时应关闭电器。门口的清楚提示能帮助师生保持习惯。其次，学校可以在普通垃圾桶旁放回收箱，并说明分类。学生就不易把有用材料与其他垃圾一起丢弃。小型宣传活动也可展示相似规则如何用于家庭垃圾。


海报撤下后，节约也应该继续。学生团体可以检查设施是否正确使用并报告问题。共享资源也是有用的一步，尤其是偶尔才使用的物品。小行动不会解决所有污染问题，但经常参与能让校园更清洁、减少浪费。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 从可执行动作开始

**骨架：** start by + doing

> The university can start by reducing unnecessary consumption and making recycling easier.

by后用动名词；两个行动形式平行。

### 2. 承认局限并指出价值

**骨架：** ...will not..., but...can...

> Small actions will not solve every pollution problem, but regular participation can make the campus cleaner and less wasteful.

不用夸大措施效果；后半句仍写具体可实现的结果。
