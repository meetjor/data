# 03 必修课程与学生选择

## 审题

讨论大学应如何在基础必修课程与学生选择之间取得平衡，明确立场和理由。

真题任务参照：2025年6月必修课必要性及2025年12月学术写作要求等立场任务；原创类题。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## Core Courses and Student Choice

A university **curriculum** should **combine** **essential** courses with **flexible** choices. Some common subjects give students the knowledge they need to become **competent** in their field. Others allow them to explore an interest and make a better **career** decision. These two purposes are both valuable.

**Professional** **qualifications** and **employment** requirements deserve attention. A student preparing for a particular occupation may need a clear foundation before taking advanced courses. At the same time, different students have different **ambitions**. Requiring everyone to follow exactly the same path may discourage useful exploration. The university could offer a small core and several **options**, with advice on how each option supports later study or work.

Choice does not mean avoiding every difficult subject. Students should explain their decisions and develop the **discipline** to finish what they start. With clear guidance, they can choose responsibly while building the abilities that their future work will require.


## 中文理解


大学课程体系应结合必要课程与灵活选择。一些共同课程提供学生胜任专业所需的知识，另一些让学生探索兴趣并作出更好的职业决定。这两个目的都有价值。


专业资格和就业要求值得重视。准备从事某种职业的学生，学习高阶课程前可能需要明确的基础。同时，不同学生有不同的志向。要求所有人遵循完全相同的道路可能妨碍有益的探索。学校可以提供一小组核心课程及若干选项，并说明每个选项如何支持后续学习或工作。


选择不等于避开所有困难科目。学生应该解释自己的决定，并培养完成所开始任务的自律。在明确指导下，他们能负责任地选择，同时建立未来工作所需的能力。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 兼顾两个目标

**骨架：** combine A with B

> A university curriculum should combine essential courses with flexible choices.

说明两个目标如何兼顾，而不是只说两者都很好。

### 2. 限制一个可能误解

**骨架：** ...does not mean + doing

> Choice does not mean avoiding every difficult subject.

mean后接avoiding这一行动；不是mean avoid。
