# 12 职业规划讲座报道

## 审题

以参加者或校报投稿者身份报告职业规划讲座的要点与收获。

真题任务参照：2023年3月职业规划讲座心得及讲座报道任务；原创场景。 不复制原题，也不把本篇说成官方范文。原题身份、任务点或指定开头与本练习不同时，以实际题干为准。正文保持120–180词；标题、称呼及落款不计入本册正文统计。

## A Career Planning Talk

Yesterday, our university invited a career adviser to give a talk about preparing for employment. The **speaker** explained how students could connect their interests with the requirements of an **occupation**. She stressed that a **realistic** plan should begin with an honest **evaluation** of our abilities.

The talk covered three useful points. First, students should investigate the **prospects** of a field before choosing a direction. Second, an **application** should describe relevant **achievements** rather than make general claims. Third, **interview** **preparation** should include clear examples of how we solved a problem or worked with others. The speaker also described how employers **recruit** staff and what information helps them make a decision.

I learned that a career **strategy** needs regular review. An early choice is a starting point, not a promise that can never change. The practical **guidance** encouraged me to compare different opportunities carefully and take one useful step now, such as improving a relevant skill or asking for advice.


## 中文理解


昨天，学校邀请一位职业顾问作就业准备讲座。主讲人解释学生如何把兴趣与某种职业的要求联系起来。她强调现实的计划应从诚实评价自身能力开始。


讲座包含三个有用要点。首先，选择方向前应调查行业前景。其次，申请材料应描述相关成就，而不是泛泛宣称。第三，面试准备应包括解决问题或与他人合作的清楚例子。主讲人也介绍雇主如何招聘，以及哪些信息有助于作决定。


我认识到职业策略需要定期复盘。早期选择是起点，不是永远不能改变的承诺。实用指导鼓励我认真比较机会，并现在采取一个有用步骤，比如提升相关技能或寻求建议。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 报告讲座讲了什么

**骨架：** The speaker explained how + 完整从句

> The speaker explained how students could connect their interests with the requirements of an occupation.

how后主语students在could前，不用问句倒装。

### 2. 报告自己的收获

**骨架：** I learned that + 完整句

> I learned that a career strategy needs regular review.

that后needs是从句谓语；不是I learned a strategy needs...再乱接。
