## Using AI as a Study Assistant

**Artificial** intelligence can be an **efficient** assistant, but it should not replace **independent** thinking. Students may use it to understand an unfamiliar idea or organize a study plan. This can leave more time for difficult **academic** tasks. The value of the tool depends on how we use it.

An answer that sounds confident is not always **accurate**. AI may **mislead** students by presenting incorrect information as a fact. It is important to check the **source** and look for **reliable** **evidence** before accepting an answer. Comparing different explanations can also help students notice mistakes. When writing an assignment, they should produce an **original** response rather than copy a complete answer.

**Responsible** use requires clear limits. AI can **assist** with preparation, while students remain responsible for the final work. They should be able to explain their own ideas without the tool. Used in this way, AI can support learning instead of becoming a shortcut around it.


## 中文理解


人工智能可以成为高效的助手，但不应代替独立思考。学生可以用它理解陌生概念或安排学习计划。这能给困难的学业任务留出更多时间。工具的价值取决于我们如何使用它。


听起来很自信的答案不一定准确。AI可能把错误信息当作事实呈现，从而误导学生。接受答案前，核查来源并寻找可靠证据很重要。比较不同解释也能帮助学生发现错误。写作业时，学生应作出原创回应，而不是复制完整答案。


负责任地使用需要明确限制。AI可以协助准备，但学生仍须对最终作业负责。他们应能在不用工具的情况下解释自己的想法。这样使用，AI就能支持学习，而不是成为绕过学习的捷径。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 承认收益后给限制

**骨架：** ...can..., but it should not...

> Artificial intelligence can be an efficient assistant, but it should not replace independent thinking.

but两边都完整；限制对应学习目的，不用一刀切。

### 2. 说明核查原则

**骨架：** It is important to + 原形 + before + doing

> It is important to check the source and look for reliable evidence before accepting an answer.

两个动作check、look并列；before后accepting是动名词。
