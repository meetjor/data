## Learning to Communicate

Good **communication** does not mean speaking more than everyone else. It means listening carefully, expressing an idea clearly and checking whether others understand it. These abilities support **collaboration** in class and help students handle **conflict** outside the classroom.

**Mutual** respect and **patience** are especially important when people disagree. Instead of quickly blaming someone, we can ask a question to **clarify** the problem. We should also **acknowledge** a useful point in another person's opinion. An **attitude** that is **sensitive** to other people's feelings can prevent a small misunderstanding from becoming a larger argument. When students **interact** with people from different backgrounds, **tolerance** helps them learn without expecting everyone to behave in the same way.

Practice can begin with an ordinary group task. Each member explains an idea, listens to comments and helps make a clear decision. Such experience can build **confidence** over time. Communication improves when students focus on solving a shared problem rather than winning every conversation.


## 中文理解


良好沟通不意味着比所有人说得更多，而是认真倾听、清楚表达，并确认他人是否理解。这些能力支持课堂合作，也帮助学生处理课堂外的冲突。


意见不同时，相互尊重和耐心尤其重要。与其立即责备某人，我们可以提问以澄清问题，也应该认可他人观点中有用的部分。体察他人的态度能防止小误解变成大争论。与不同背景的人交流时，宽容能帮助学生学习，而不是期待所有人行为一样。


实践可以从普通小组任务开始。每位成员解释想法、听取意见，并帮助作出明确决定。这些经历能逐步建立信心。学生关注解决共同问题，而不是赢得每段对话时，沟通就会改善。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 澄清概念

**骨架：** ...does not mean + doing

> Good communication does not mean speaking more than everyone else.

先排除常见误解，再说明真正要做的事。

### 2. 替代不合适的行动

**骨架：** Instead of + doing, we can + 原形

> Instead of quickly blaming someone, we can ask a question to clarify the problem.

instead of后是动名词短语，不直接放完整句。
