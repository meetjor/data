## 审题

说明大学生保持身心健康的重要性，并提出可实行的日常做法。

## Keeping a Balanced Student Life

**Psychological** health is as important as **physical** health in college life. Students often notice illness but ignore **anxiety**, **stress** or constant **fatigue**. A **balanced** **routine** can help them study more steadily and enjoy their time at university. Looking after health should be part of daily life, not only a response to a serious problem.

A regular **diet**, enough rest and suitable exercise provide a useful starting point. Students also need time to **relax** and talk with someone they trust. These habits may improve their **mood** and help them **concentrate** on learning tasks. When difficulties continue, asking for appropriate support is better than pretending that everything is fine.

Balance does not require a perfect timetable. A small, realistic change is easier to maintain than a strict plan that soon fails. Friends can **motivate** one another to keep helpful habits. By paying attention to both mind and body, students can build a healthier foundation for learning and future work.


## 中文理解


心理健康与身体健康在大学生活中同样重要。学生常注意疾病，却忽视焦虑、压力或持续疲劳。均衡的日常安排能帮助他们更稳定地学习并享受大学时光。照顾健康应是日常生活的一部分，不只是在严重问题出现后的应对。


规律饮食、足够休息和适当运动是有用的起点。学生也需要放松，并与信任的人交谈。这些习惯可能改善心情，帮助他们专注学习任务。困难持续时，寻求合适支持比假装一切正常更好。


均衡不需要完美时间表。现实的小改变比很快失败的严格计划更易保持。朋友可以相互激励，坚持有益习惯。关注身心两方面，学生就能为学习和未来工作建立更健康的基础。


## 两个重点结构

### 1. 说明同等重要

**骨架：** A is as important as B

> Psychological health is as important as physical health in college life.

as...as中间用形容词原级，不写more important。

### 2. 把重要性落实到行动

**骨架：** ...should be part of...

> Looking after health should be part of daily life, not only a response to a serious problem.

行动短语Looking after health作主语，明确应该在何时做。
