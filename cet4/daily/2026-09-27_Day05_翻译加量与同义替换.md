## 五、基础语法与句型｜20分钟

### 1. one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词

✅ **Tieguanyin is one of the most popular teas in China.**

注意：
- one of the 后面虽然是“其中一个”，后面的可数名词仍然用复数：**teas**。

### 2. help + 动词原形

✅ Drinking tea can **help prevent** disease.
✅ Good habits can **help reduce** mistakes.

基础阶段继续统一使用：**help + 动词原形**。

### 3. student / students 与 need / needs

✅ **Students need** to practice.
✅ **A student needs** to practice.

> 以后写作文时先看主语是单数还是复数，再决定 need / needs。

### 4. if 从句也必须有主语

✅ **If students make mistakes, they should correct them.**

不要写：

❌ If make mistakes, they should correct it.

两个点：
- if 后面仍然需要完整的“谁 + 做什么”；
- mistakes 是复数，后面用 **them** 更合适。

# 九、阅读｜最后｜2021年6月第1套 Passage Two 第55题

今天完成这篇 Passage Two 的最后一题。重点继续练 **不要只找长得一样的词，要找意思一样的表达**。

### 定位句

> Therefore, businesses need to set up their IT infrastructure so that it is designed to **fit in with their employees' work**, rather than **adjust their work to fit in with the company's IT limitations**.

先理解两个表达：

- **fit in with...** — 与……相适应 / 符合……
- **rather than...** — 而不是……

### 第55题

**How can a business help improve its employees' experience in using IT?**

A. By designing systems that suit their needs.
B. By ensuring that their mental health is sound.
C. By adjusting their work to suit the IT system.
D. By offering them regular in-service training.

我的答案：______

把握：□ 高  □ 中  □ 低

我认为的原文对应：____________________________________________

### 今天特别防一个陷阱

原文说的是：

**让 IT 系统适应员工的工作**，而不是 **让员工的工作去适应 IT 系统**。

所以做题时要看清“谁适应谁”，不能只因为选项里出现 `work / IT / suit` 就选。
