# 五、基础语法与句型｜20分钟

## 1. 被动：be + 过去分词

Day 05 你写“产于……”时用了 `in produce`。

正确结构：

> **Tea is produced in Fujian.**

结构：

**主语 + be + 过去分词 + 其他**

再看：

> **The research is funded by the food industry.**

- is produced = 被生产
- is funded = 被资助

今天只要求能认、能套简单句。

---

## 2. depend on = 取决于

> The price depends **on** the quality.

不要漏掉 **on**。

---

## 3. rather than = 而不是

Day 05 阅读55题就是在这里丢分。

> Students should check the facts **rather than** guess.

意思：

> 学生应该核实事实，**而不是**猜。

看到 rather than 时，必须先判断：
**作者肯定前面，还是肯定后面？**

---

## 4. The more..., the more...｜先认识

四级翻译里经常出现“越……越……”。

> **The longer tea is brewed, the stronger its flavor becomes.**

先把它理解成：

> 茶泡得越久，味道越浓。

今天不要求你完全自由造句，但翻译时会尝试一次。

### 可以使用

- In my opinion, ...
- Technology can help people...
- However, ...
- If people depend on technology too much, ...
- We should make good use of technology.
- All in all, ...

### 今天重点

不要硬写复杂关系从句。

> Technology is useful.  
> It can help students find information quickly.

- □ need / needs
- □ a / an
- □ depend on
