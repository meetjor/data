- Technology can **affect** learning.（动词）

✅ Students should avoid **depending** on one source.  
❌ avoid depend on...

## 3. allow / require + 人 + to do

- Technology allows students **to find** information quickly.
- Some tasks require students **to think** carefully.

# 九、电子阅读｜最后

## Why the First Answer Feels Right

**原创四级风格电子阅读｜约310词｜第一遍不要查词**

When students search for information online, many of them stop after finding the first answer that seems reasonable. This saves time, but it can also lead to poor decisions.

A small classroom experiment showed how easily this can happen. A teacher gave forty students a question about study methods. Half of the students first read an article saying that studying late at night was highly effective. The other half first read an article saying the opposite.

Then all students received the same four pieces of evidence. Two supported late-night study, while two suggested that it could reduce attention the next day.

The interesting part was what happened next.

Students tended to remember the evidence that supported the first article they had read. They were also more likely to describe that evidence as “strong” or “reliable.” Evidence that challenged their first idea was often judged more critically.

The teacher explained that people do not always compare information in a completely objective way. Our first impression can **influence** how we understand later information.

This does not mean the first answer is always wrong. It means we should be careful before reaching a conclusion.

One simple method is to ask three questions:

1. What is the source?
2. What evidence supports the claim?
3. Is there another reasonable explanation?

Taking a few extra minutes to compare different sources may feel slow, but it can improve the quality of a decision.
